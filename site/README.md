# Genix site

Static pages (Russian at the root, English in `en/`): landing, terms of use, privacy policy, refunds. Built by `python3 tools/make_site.py`; edit the texts there, not the HTML.

Before publishing, fill every `[PLACEHOLDER]` in the legal pages (seller, address, support e-mail, jurisdiction, dates, hosting, retention). The legal texts are templates written without a lawyer and must be reviewed by one. `img/library.jpg` is a screenshot of Genix 0.00.46 and `img/glass.png` a rendered model; retake them after big UI changes. The logo is the current app icon (`crates/app/assets/logo.png`).
