An honest note on capabilities

Geometry is mesh-based rather than exact surfaces: STEP and DXF export is faceted. That suits small parts, 3D printing and CNC sketches; it does not suit complex assemblies or exact freeform surfaces. The app is verified on macOS (Apple Silicon); builds for other systems are produced on GitHub but were not tested by us.
